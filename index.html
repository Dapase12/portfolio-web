Featured ML Thesis · 2026

Malaria Triage & Phenotyping with Machine Learning

Clinical problem: Identify which Plasmodium falciparum patients face the highest risk of severe outcomes so triage and treatment can be prioritized at a Venezuelan reference center.

Technical stack

Python scikit-learn CART · PAM R Geospatial analysis

Results: Retrospective cohort (n=570, 2017–2026). Three clinical phenotypes identified; CART accuracy 85.4% (95% CI: 82.4–88.1%); sensitivity 93.5% for the highest-risk group.
